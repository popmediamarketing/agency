Chamuel Setting Library System

Naming rule:
Do not use Ring 01 / Ring 02.
Use proper Chamuel names and CC codes.

Example:
- Aurora — CC-A001
- Harmony — CC-HH001
- Celeste — CC-C001
- Nova — CC-N001
- Cathedral One — CC-Co001

Scoring categories:
1. Sparkle
2. Maintenance
3. Durability
4. Daily Wear
5. Cleaning Difficulty
6. Resizing
7. Budget Efficiency

Score meaning:
★★★★★ = strongest / easiest / best
★★★☆☆ = balanced / moderate
★☆☆☆☆ = weak / difficult / less suitable

Important:
For Maintenance and Cleaning Difficulty, higher score means easier to maintain or clean.
