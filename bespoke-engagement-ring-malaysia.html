What bespoke means

Bespoke does not always mean complicated. It means the ring direction is planned around the wearer, the story and the budget. The design can be as simple as a clean solitaire with a hidden detail, or as detailed as a symbolic custom setting.

A meaningful ring should still be durable and comfortable for daily wear.

Design process

The process usually starts with a reference image or style direction. We then advise on stone type, diamond shape, setting, metal and feasibility before quotation.

Once direction and deposit are confirmed, the ring enters design and production.

Best bespoke details

Popular bespoke details include hidden halos, engraved dates, birthstones, special prong design, symbolic side lines and custom band profiles.

Hidden details

Add a private meaning that only the couple knows.

Shape selection

Choose round, oval, emerald, heart or other diamond shapes.

Metal planning

Match 9K, 14K, 18K or platinum to the budget and design.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is bespoke engagement ring expensive?

It depends on the design, stone and material. A bespoke direction can be simple or premium.

Can I hide a symbol inside the design?

Yes, hidden details or engraving can be planned if technically suitable.

Do I need a clear design before asking?

No. A reference image and budget are enough to start the discussion.
