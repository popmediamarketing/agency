Chamuel & Co. — Archangel Chamuel Palette Trial

Color direction used:
- Deep Rose Wine: #5b2638 / #7f3548
- Rose Quartz: #c7768a
- Soft Blush: #fff6f4 / #f5d8dd
- Champagne Gold: #c6a467 / #f3ddb2

Design intention:
- Replace heavy black sections with softer rose/champagne gradients.
- Keep a premium jewelry feel, not too sweet.
- Retain gold as luxury accent.
- Use deep rose instead of black for contrast and readability.

Changed areas:
- Top announcement bar
- Main dark buttons
- CTA panels
- Footer
- Advisor note blocks
- General background and alternate section background
