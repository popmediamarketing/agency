What is a lab-grown diamond?

A lab-grown diamond is a diamond created in a controlled lab environment. It has pure carbon composition and diamond hardness, similar to natural diamond, but its origin is different.

This makes it attractive for customers who want a real diamond direction while keeping budget flexibility.

Why customers choose lab-grown diamonds

The most common reason is size-to-budget value. Customers can often explore a larger stone or better specifications compared with natural diamond at the same budget.

Lab-grown diamonds are especially popular for engagement rings, solitaire designs, pavé rings and modern custom jewelry.

What to compare

Compare cut, colour, clarity, carat, certification, shape and setting. The final ring still depends on the gold material, ring design and craftsmanship.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is a lab-grown diamond a real diamond?

It has diamond composition and diamond hardness, but it is created in a lab instead of mined from the earth.

Is lab-grown diamond cheaper than natural diamond?

Generally, lab-grown diamonds are more budget-flexible than natural diamonds of similar specifications.

Can lab-grown diamonds come with certificate?

Yes, many lab-grown diamonds can come with certification depending on stone selection.
