Couple rings for different occasions

Couple rings are suitable for dating anniversaries, weddings, promise rings, birthdays and meaningful milestones. The design can be simple or personalised depending on how often the rings will be worn.

For daily wear, comfort and material choice matter more than overly complicated design.

Silver or gold couple rings

Silver is practical for a lower budget or symbolic gift. 9K gold is a good starting point for real gold. 14K and 18K gold are better for customers who want stronger long-term value and durability.

The price for a pair depends heavily on both ring sizes and the selected width.

Design ideas

Popular options include plain bands, matching lines, different widths, small stones, date engraving, initials, heartbeat lines or hidden messages inside the rings.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Can couple rings be made in silver?

Yes, silver is suitable for entry-level couple rings and symbolic gifts.

Can we make one ring wider than the other?

Yes, many couples choose different widths while keeping the same design concept.

How long do custom couple rings take?

Most custom work takes around 14–21 working days after confirmation.
