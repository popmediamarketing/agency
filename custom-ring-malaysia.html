Custom ring design direction

A custom ring can be simple or detailed, but the best design starts with the wearer’s lifestyle. Someone who works with their hands may need a smoother, more secure setting, while someone who prefers sparkle may like pavé or halo designs.

The first step is not to choose the most complicated design, but to choose what is practical and meaningful.

Material choices

Silver is suitable for entry-level symbolic pieces. 9K gold can begin a real-gold direction. 14K gold is balanced for daily wear, while 18K gold feels more premium. Platinum may be discussed for higher-end durability.

The ring size, width and thickness all affect the final price.

Stone choices

Custom rings can use moissanite, lab-grown diamond, natural diamond or selected gemstones depending on design and budget. Each option should be explained clearly before confirmation.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Can I customise ring size?

Yes, ring size should be confirmed as accurately as possible before production.

Can I adjust width and thickness?

Yes, but changes may affect weight, comfort and price.

Can custom rings be made urgently?

Urgent requests depend on design and production schedule.
