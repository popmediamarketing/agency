Proposal ring planning

A good proposal ring does not need to be the most expensive option. It should be the right balance of meaning, durability, beauty and timing.

If the proposal date is near, production timeline becomes a major factor. A custom ring normally needs planning time, while selected ready-stock pieces may be faster if available.

Stone options for proposal rings

Natural diamonds are valued for earth-mined origin and tradition. Lab-grown diamonds are popular for customers who want diamond composition with better size-to-budget flexibility. Moissanite gives a strong sparkling look at a more accessible budget.

Each option can be beautiful, but the explanation should be honest so the customer understands what they are buying.

What to send us

Send your proposal date, rough budget, preferred design style, reference image and ring size if available. We can then advise whether the direction is more suitable for silver, 9K gold, 14K gold or 18K gold.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Can I propose with moissanite?

Yes, moissanite is suitable when the priority is sparkle and a more comfortable starting budget.

Should I choose natural or lab-grown diamond?

Choose natural for earth-mined tradition; choose lab-grown for diamond composition with better size flexibility.

Can I customise from a reference photo?

Yes. We can use a reference photo to understand the style and then advise what is practical for your budget.
