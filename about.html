About Chamuel & Co.

Where love becomes something you can keep.

Chamuel & Co. was inspired by the meaning of love, protection and the people who matter most — family, romance, friendship and the memories that stay with us.

Our Name

Inspired by Archangel Chamuel, the angel of love.

The name Chamuel carries the feeling of love, peace, connection and protection. For us, jewelry is not only about diamond and gold. It is about the people we love, the relationships we treasure, and the memories we hope to keep close.

Family love, romantic love and friendship are all part of a person’s life story. Chamuel & Co. exists to turn those stories into jewelry that can be worn, kept and passed on.

The Moment We Understood

Jewelry was never just jewelry.

When I was young, I never fully understood why the elders in our family cared so deeply about jewelry.

To me, they were beautiful pieces — gold rings, necklaces, something worn on the hand or around the neck. But I did not yet understand why they were kept so carefully, why they were spoken about with such emotion, and why they seemed to carry a meaning beyond their value.

Until the day my grandfather left us.

At the hospital, he took the gold ring he had always worn and personally passed it to the person he loved the most. His necklace was left for his children.

In that moment, I finally understood.

Jewelry can be a meaning. A chapter of life. A memory. A form of protection. A family’s story. A quiet way for love to continue staying beside the people we care about.

My grandmother would also pass down some of the jewelry she often wore. Whenever those pieces came to us, we kept them carefully — not because they were only accessories, but because one day, they would become time that could never be bought again.

They are proof that someone we love once lived, once loved us, and in a quiet way, still stays with us.
