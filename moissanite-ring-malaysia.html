What makes moissanite different

Moissanite is not a diamond. It is a different gemstone known for strong fire and colourful flashes. Some customers love the rainbow sparkle, while others prefer the classic white sparkle of diamonds.

The key is to choose it honestly and understand the difference before buying.

When moissanite is suitable

Moissanite works well for entry-level proposal rings, daily rings, earrings, necklaces and custom gifts. It is especially useful when the customer wants a larger stone look but does not want to increase the budget too much.

It can be paired with silver, 9K gold, 14K gold or 18K gold depending on the purpose.

Moissanite vs lab-grown diamond

Lab-grown diamond has diamond composition and classic white sparkle. Moissanite has silicon carbide composition and more rainbow fire. Price direction normally starts with moissanite, then lab-grown diamond, then natural diamond.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is moissanite a diamond?

No. Moissanite is a different gemstone with a different composition and sparkle style.

Does moissanite sparkle more than diamond?

Moissanite often shows stronger rainbow fire, while diamond has a more classic white sparkle.

Is moissanite suitable for daily wear?

It can be suitable for daily wear with proper design and care.
