Why choose natural diamond

Natural diamonds are earth-mined and valued for rarity, tradition and symbolic meaning. Many customers choose natural diamond because they want a classic fine-jewelry direction.

It is usually the premium-priced direction compared with moissanite and lab-grown diamond.

What affects natural diamond price

Carat, cut, colour, clarity, certification, shape and availability can all affect price. The ring setting and gold material add to the final quotation.

Because natural diamond prices can vary widely, it is important to decide what matters most: size, quality, origin or overall ring design.

Suitable designs

Natural diamonds are often used in solitaire rings, classic engagement rings, anniversary rings, earrings and heirloom-style jewelry. A clean setting helps showcase the stone.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is natural diamond more expensive?

Usually yes, because natural diamonds are earth-mined and priced by rarity, specifications and demand.

Should I choose natural or lab-grown?

Choose natural for earth-mined origin and tradition; choose lab-grown for modern diamond value and size flexibility.

Can natural diamond rings be customised?

Yes, the setting, metal, shape and design details can be customised.
