Why choose 18K gold for wedding bands

18K gold is a premium material direction with higher gold content. It is suitable for couples who want their wedding bands to feel more valuable and meaningful for long-term wearing.

It can be used for plain bands, textured bands, diamond bands and personalised couple rings.

Budget planning

Our general material guide places RM2,800–RM4,000 in an 18K gold direction depending on ring size, width, thickness and design. A pair of rings may require higher budget depending on both sizes and total gold weight.

For wedding bands, small changes in width and thickness can affect price noticeably.

Design choices

18K wedding bands can be classic, matte, brushed, hammered, engraved, curved or diamond-accented. The best design should match daily lifestyle.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is 18K gold good for wedding bands?

Yes, it is a premium fine-jewelry material and suitable for meaningful wedding bands.

Can we customise 18K couple rings?

Yes, width, texture, engraving and design details can be customised.

Why does 18K cost more?

It has higher gold content and final price also depends on weight and craftsmanship.
