How custom jewelry works

Custom jewelry starts with your purpose. It may be a proposal, wedding, anniversary, birthday, family gift or self-reward. The occasion helps decide the material, stone type, budget and timeline.

A design can start from a sketch, a reference photo, a word, a symbol or a story. Our role is to translate it into something wearable and realistic.

What can be customised

Rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings, pendants and selected gemstone pieces can be customised depending on feasibility. The design can include engraving, stone choice, metal choice, shape, size and finishing.

Not every reference photo can be copied exactly. We advise on what is practical for the budget and daily wear.

Budget and timeline

Custom jewelry usually requires confirmation before production. Most pieces take around 14–21 working days, depending on design complexity and production queue.

A clear budget range helps us recommend silver, 9K, 14K, 18K gold or platinum more accurately.
