Stone Comparison

Moissanite vs Lab-Grown Diamond vs Natural Diamond.

Use this guide to understand the real difference before choosing a proposal ring, wedding jewelry or custom piece.

01

Moissanite

Moissanite is not a diamond. It is made from silicon carbide and is known for stronger rainbow fire. It is usually the most budget-friendly direction when the customer wants a bigger, sparkling look.

  • Hardness around 9.25
  • Lab created
  • More colourful flashes
  • Usually most affordable
02

Lab-Grown Diamond

Lab-grown diamond has the same pure carbon composition as natural diamond, but it is created in a lab. It gives classic white diamond sparkle with better size-to-budget flexibility.

  • Diamond hardness 10
  • Pure carbon
  • Classic white sparkle
  • Modern proposal choice
03

Natural Diamond

Natural diamond is pure carbon formed by nature and mined from the earth. It is usually the most expensive direction because customers are paying for origin, rarity and tradition.

  • Diamond hardness 10
  • Earth mined
  • Classic white sparkle
  • Traditional value
Advisor explanation: Lab-grown diamond and natural diamond are both diamonds in composition. The main difference is origin: lab-created versus earth-mined. Moissanite is a different gemstone, with stronger rainbow fire and a lower price direction. Cost direction is usually Moissanite < Lab-Grown Diamond < Natural Diamond.

How the stone is held changes the entire look.
