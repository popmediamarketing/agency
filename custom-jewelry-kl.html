Custom jewelry for KL customers

Customers in KL often look for custom jewelry when ready-made designs feel too common. Chamuel & Co. helps turn references and personal stories into wearable jewelry.

The process can start through WhatsApp before arranging a visit or delivery.

What to prepare

Send your budget, purpose, reference image, preferred material and timeline. This allows us to advise whether the piece is suitable for silver, 9K, 14K, 18K gold or platinum.

If the design involves diamonds or gemstones, stone type and size will affect quotation.

Suitable custom pieces

Popular requests include engagement rings, couple rings, name necklaces, anniversary pendants, gemstone rings and family jewelry.
