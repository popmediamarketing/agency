Why choose a custom name necklace

Name necklaces are meaningful because they carry identity, memory or relationship. They can be made for partners, children, mothers, birthdays or self-reward.

The design should be readable, wearable and suitable for the customer’s daily style.

Material direction

Silver is suitable for entry budget. Gold options such as 9K, 14K and 18K can be considered for longer-term wear and higher value.

The price depends on chain type, name length, thickness, material and any added stones.

Design details

Name necklaces can be classic script, clean block letters, initials, birthstone accents or meaningful symbol combinations.
