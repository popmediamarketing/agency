Why engagement ring prices vary

Two rings can look similar in photos but be priced differently because of stone type, diamond specs, gold karat, gold weight, setting style and craftsmanship.

A simple solitaire ring may cost less than a ring with side stones, hidden halo or complex custom structure.

Budget range guide

RM300–RM1,500 generally points toward silver or entry symbolic jewelry. RM1,500–RM2,000 can start a 9K gold direction. RM2,000–RM2,800 can explore 14K gold. RM2,800–RM4,000 can explore 18K gold depending on design.

For diamond proposal rings, the final budget also depends on whether you choose moissanite, lab-grown diamond or natural diamond.

How to avoid overspending

Start with your comfortable budget and the proposal date. Then adjust stone size, stone type, gold karat, ring thickness or setting detail to match the budget.

Lower budget

Silver or simple symbolic designs.

Middle budget

9K or 14K gold with controlled design.

Higher budget

18K gold, lab-grown diamond or premium custom direction.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Can RM800 make a gold ring?

Some very simple 9K gold directions may be possible, but for website recommendation we keep RM1,500+ as a more practical starting guide.

What is the best budget for proposal ring?

It depends on expectations. A clear budget helps us recommend the right material and stone type.

Is moissanite cheaper than diamond?

Generally yes, moissanite is usually more budget-friendly than lab-grown and natural diamond.
