Typical custom ring timeline

Most custom jewelry takes around 14–21 working days depending on design complexity, material, stone arrangement and production queue.

Simple designs may be faster, while detailed rings with many stones, special settings or design revisions may require more time.

What happens before production

Before production, the design direction, budget, material, stone type and size need to be clarified. A deposit is usually required before custom work begins.

The clearer the reference and requirements, the smoother the process becomes.

How to avoid delay

Prepare your reference photos, ring size, proposal or wedding date, material preference and budget early. Last-minute changes can affect production timeline.

Planning stage

Clarify design, stone, material and budget.

Production stage

Crafting and setting based on confirmed direction.

Final check

Inspection, cleaning and collection arrangement.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Can custom rings be rushed?

Rush orders depend on design and production schedule.

Do design changes affect timeline?

Yes, changes after confirmation can extend the timeline.

Should I order before proposal date?

Yes, plan ahead whenever possible to allow enough time for production and checking.
