What affects lab-grown diamond price

Carat, cut, colour, clarity, shape and certification affect the stone price. The ring price also includes setting style, gold material, ring size, gold weight and craftsmanship.

A larger stone with lower specs may not be better than a smaller stone with stronger cut and design balance.

Ring setting cost

A solitaire ring is usually simpler than a pavé, hidden halo or three-stone design. Extra small diamonds and detailed setting work increase production cost.

Gold karat also matters. 14K and 18K gold have different cost and positioning.

How to compare properly

Do not compare only carat size. Compare full specifications, design, material, warranty, after-sales and whether the ring is suitable for daily wear.

Stone specs

Carat, cut, colour, clarity and shape.

Ring material

9K, 14K, 18K gold or platinum.

Craftsmanship

Setting work, finishing and custom details.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you decide.

Is lab-grown diamond cheaper than natural diamond?

Generally yes for similar specifications, but price still depends on the stone and ring design.

Does ring setting affect price?

Yes, more detailed settings require more work and sometimes more stones.

Can I set a lab-grown diamond in 18K gold?

Yes, 18K gold is a premium direction for lab-grown diamond rings.
